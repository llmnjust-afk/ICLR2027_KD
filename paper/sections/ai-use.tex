\section{AI Use Statement}
\label{sec:ai-use}

In this work, we used generative AI tools (LLMs) for: (1)~implementing and debugging the CAGS complexity analysis pipeline, dataset generation scripts, and classifier training code; (2)~formatting LaTeX proofs and mathematical derivations; (3)~drafting and editing portions of the manuscript for clarity; and (4)~formatting references. We did not use AI tools for generating experimental results, fabricating data, or drawing scientific conclusions. All experimental design, data analysis, theoretical development, and interpretation of results were conducted solely by the authors. We have verified the correctness of all AI-assisted code, proofs, and text, and take responsibility for the final content.
