\begin{abstract}
Dataset distillation seeks to compress large-scale datasets into compact synthetic surrogates that preserve training utility. Recent training-free approaches leveraging pretrained diffusion models, exemplified by MGD$^3$, achieve strong performance by synthesizing class-conditional images without optimizing the generator. However, these methods apply a globally fixed classifier-free guidance (CFG) strength across all classes, ignoring that different classes exhibit varying structural complexity. We propose \ags{} (Adaptive Guidance Scheduling for Diffusion Dataset Distillation), a training-free framework whose core module, \cags{} (Class-Adaptive Guidance Strength), assigns per-class guidance based on a multi-factor complexity metric combining mode count, intra-class variance, and inter-class separability. Two complementary modules address secondary axes of variation: \iast{} (IPC-Adaptive Guidance Range) scales the guidance range as a function of the images-per-class budget, and \tags{} (Timestep-Adaptive Guidance Schedule) modulates guidance across diffusion timesteps. All modules operate at inference time on a frozen pretrained DiT, adding zero trainable parameters. Experiments on ImageNet-100, ImageNette, and ImageWoof across ConvNet-6, ResNet-18, and ResNetAP-10 demonstrate that \cags{} yields consistent improvements across all IPC settings: $+10.4\%$ relative at IPC=10 and $+15.5\%$ at IPC=50 on the 10-class subset, and $+7.4\%$ at IPC=10 and $+19.4\%$ at IPC=50 on the 100-class subset. \cags{} also surpasses the best fixed $\lambda$ on the 10-class subset ($50.13\%$ vs.\ $48.47\%$), demonstrating that per-class adaptation is beneficial even when global guidance is well-tuned. Critically, fixed guidance \emph{hurts} performance on the 100-class subset ($-1.9\%$ relative), where class complexity variation is largest, while \cags{} overcomes this limitation. \iast{} provides essential protection for the hardest setting---100-class IPC=1---where full-range \cags{} barely improves over unguided ($+1.6\%$) but \iast{}-scaled \cags{} achieves $+11.2\%$ relative. The improvements are architecture-agnostic, with \cags{} yielding $+13.8\%$ on ResNet-18 and $+15.2\%$ on ResNetAP-10. These results, obtained under the MGD$^3$-matched evaluation protocol with best-accuracy tracking, confirm that per-class adaptive guidance is essential for scaling diffusion-based dataset distillation.
\end{abstract}
