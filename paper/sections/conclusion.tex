\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented \cags{}, a training-free method that replaces MGD$^3$'s globally fixed classifier-free guidance with per-class adaptive guidance based on cluster entropy and intra-class variance. A systematic factor ablation from four factors identified this two-factor configuration as optimal. Theoretically, $\lambda_c^* \propto v_c / \sigma_c^2$ is increasing in both intra-class variance and entropy, and the adaptivity gap grows with cross-class heterogeneity. On ImageNet-100, \cags{} surpasses all published baselines on all three architectures ($30.81\%$ on ResNet-18, $29.74\%$ on ResNetAP-10, $25.55\%$ on ConvNet-6), with the advantage growing to $+31$--$+44\%$ relative at 1000 classes. The benefit depends on cross-class complexity variation rather than class count alone: neutral on the 10-class ImageNet-100 subset but $+5.28\%$ on ImageNette. \cags{} preserves the training-free, single-pass paradigm of MGD$^3$ with negligible overhead, and is orthogonal to distribution-matching and sample-selection approaches.
