\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The two-factor configuration $(\alpha{=}0, \beta{=}0.5, \gamma{=}0.5, \delta{=}0)$ is the recommended default; the four-factor design may be preferred on datasets with high mode-count or separability variation. More seeds would provide stronger statistical guarantees. Our ImageNette baseline ($54.0\%$) is lower than DMGD's~\cite{wang2026dmgd} ($59.1\%$), likely due to heavier augmentation. The analysis assumes well-separated Gaussian modes and second-order approximation; a more general analysis is future work. Non-ImageNet results use SD~1.5, yielding low absolute accuracy on Food-101 ($4.17\%$). All hyperparameters are in Section~\ref{sec:setup}; code will be released upon acceptance. LLMs were used for code implementation, proof formatting, and manuscript editing; all experimental design and conclusions are solely the authors'. Full details in the AI Use Statement and Appendix~\ref{sec:reproducibility}.
