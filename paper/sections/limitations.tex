\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We identify several limitations of the current work that point to directions for future investigation.

\paragraph{Boundary conditions for \cags{}.}
\cags{} provides substantial gains when class complexity variation is large (100-class subset, $+7.6\%$ to $+14.8\%$ relative) but is neutral or harmful when variation is limited (10-class IPC=1, $-12.8\%$; ImageWoof, $-5.2\%$). This boundary condition is inherent to the per-class adaptation approach: when all classes share similar structural complexity, per-class guidance differentiation provides no benefit and may reduce intra-class diversity. Practitioners should verify that complexity scores exhibit meaningful variation before applying \cags{}.

\paragraph{\iast{} and \tags{} are not recommended.}
\iast{}'s log-scaling is counterproductive in both tested IPC=1 settings: it eliminates \cags{}'s benefit on 100-class ($5.87\%$ vs.\ $6.57\%$) and only partially recovers \cags{}'s degradation on 10-class ($21.13\%$ vs.\ $19.87\%$), with both remaining below unguided ($22.80\%$). \tags{} does not improve over \cags{}-alone. A class-count-conditioned scaling function for \iast{} and more sophisticated temporal schedules for \tags{} are directions for future work.

\paragraph{Fixed complexity weights.}
The multi-factor complexity metric uses fixed weights $(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \delta) = (0.15, 0.15, 0.35, 0.35)$ that are not learned from data. While our experiments demonstrate that these weights yield consistent improvements on the 100-class subset, learning the weights---or conducting a systematic sensitivity analysis---could further optimize the complexity metric for specific dataset characteristics.

\paragraph{Evaluation scope.}
Our experiments use three random seeds per configuration. While the improvements are consistent across seeds with tight standard deviations, a larger-scale evaluation with more seeds would provide stronger statistical guarantees. Additionally, we evaluate on ImageNet-100, ImageNette, and ImageWoof; extending to the full ImageNet-1K and other large-scale datasets would further validate the approach's scalability.

\paragraph{Single generator.}
All experiments use the DiT-XL/2 model~\cite{peebles2022scalable} pretrained on ImageNet-1K. While the adaptive guidance principle is architecture-agnostic, evaluating \ags{} with other pretrained diffusion models (e.g., Stable Diffusion, class-conditional U-Net diffusion) would demonstrate broader applicability.
